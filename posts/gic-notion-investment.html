Singapore's GIC Invests $270 Million in AI Workspace Leader Notion

Date: February 25, 2026

Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC has made a significant bet on the future of AI-powered productivity tools, investing in Notion through a landmark US$270 million employee share sale that values the company at US$11 billion. This marks GIC's first investment in the AI workspace player, signaling growing institutional appetite for AI-driven enterprise solutions.

A Strategic Partnership for the AI Era

The investment, completed alongside returning investors Sequoia Capital and Index Ventures, represents more than just a financial transaction. Notion specifically called GIC an "obvious choice" as an investment partner, citing the sovereign wealth fund's expertise in supporting growth across Asia-Pacific and its commitment to long-term value creation.

"One of the reasons we're so thrilled to partner with GIC is because of their expertise in supporting growth in Asia-Pacific and across the globe, as well as their commitment to the long term. They think in decades, not quarters," Notion stated, highlighting the alignment between the company's vision and GIC's investment philosophy.

Notion's Singapore Connection

The investment comes amid Notion's aggressive expansion in the Asia-Pacific region. The company opened a Singapore office in mid-2025 as part of its regional push, joining other Asia-Pacific offices in Tokyo, Seoul, and Sydney. This strategic positioning has proven timely, with the company experiencing "explosive growth" in the region.

Notion co-founder Akshay Kothari highlighted the company's strong Asia-Pacific performance in a LinkedIn post, noting that the region's growth was a key factor in securing GIC's partnership. The timing aligns with Notion's broader strategy to capture market share in one of the world's fastest-growing tech regions.

AI Transformation Driving Valuation

Notion's impressive valuation of US$11 billion reflects the company's successful integration of AI capabilities into its platform. The company reported that at the end of last year, more than 50 percent of its annual recurring revenue came from AI-enabled customers—a figure that has more than doubled over the past year.

This metrics underscores how AI has become a core driver of Notion's business model, transforming from a simple note-taking and project management tool into an intelligent workspace that helps teams organize, collaborate, and automate workflows. The company's AI features, including smart summaries, automated categorization, and intelligent search, have resonated strongly with enterprise customers.

Employee Liquidity and Growth Momentum

The tender offer, which allowed current and former employees to sell their vested shares, also demonstrated Notion's commitment to employee welfare. The company removed the one-year vesting cliff on options, enabling nearly all employees to participate in the tender. This move reflects a growing trend among tech companies to provide earlier liquidity to employees while maintaining long-term incentives.

The successful completion of this funding round, despite challenging market conditions for technology investments, speaks to Notion's strong fundamentals and the confidence that leading investors place in its AI-powered vision.

What This Means for Singapore's Tech Ecosystem

GIC's investment in Notion highlights Singapore's dual role as both a hub for AI innovation and a gateway for global capital to access promising technology companies. The sovereign wealth fund's continued activity in the AI space—from investments in AI startups to supporting established players—reinforces Singapore's position as a leading destination for technology investments in Asia.

For Singapore's growing AI ecosystem, the Notion investment serves as another data point demonstrating that global capital recognizes the value being created in the region. As more companies establish presence in Singapore to access the broader Asia-Pacific market, such investments are likely to accelerate.
